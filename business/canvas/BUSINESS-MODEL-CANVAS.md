# SkillCo Business Model Canvas

## Executive Summary

SkillCo is a platform that transforms individual skills into productized companies. Think of it as the App Store for the world of work — a marketplace of skills-turned-products plus the infrastructure to build, design, ship, and operate them.

---

## The Nine Building Blocks

### 1. Customer Segments

**Primary: Skill Creators (Supply Side)**
- **Solo developers** with technical skills but no product/business process
- **Consultants** who want to productize their expertise
- **Creators** (course makers, tool builders) who lack shipping infrastructure
- **AI power users** who've built useful workflows/skills

**Secondary: Skill Consumers (Demand Side)**
- **SMBs** needing specialized tools without enterprise budgets
- **Freelancers** looking for productivity multipliers
- **Teams** that need niche solutions not covered by big SaaS
- **Enterprises** scouting innovative solutions from the edge

**Tertiary: Agent Teams (Internal)**
- Businesses wanting full-service agent teams to run operations

---

### 2. Value Propositions

| Segment | Value Proposition |
|---------|-------------------|
| **Skill Creators** | Turn your skill into a working product + company in one process. We handle design, deployment, payments, and give you an agent team to run it. |
| **Skill Consumers** | Access a marketplace of specialized tools built by experts in every niche. Better than generic SaaS, cheaper than custom dev. |
| **Agent Team Buyers** | Get a pre-configured team of AI agents to run your business operations — like hiring a department without the overhead. |

**Core Differentiator:** The full stack. Others help you build OR sell OR run. We do all three as one integrated flow.

**Fixed Costs:**
- Engineering salaries (60% of costs)
- Cloud infrastructure baseline
- Office / tools / SaaS

**Variable Costs:**

## Top 3 Risks & Mitigations

### Risk 1: Supply-Side Chicken-and-Egg
**Risk:** Marketplace needs skills to attract buyers, but creators won't come without buyers.
**Mitigation:** 
- Seed marketplace with internal skills (Artist Vault, etc.)
- Creator-first strategy: make the builder valuable standalone
- Partner with existing creators to port their products

### Risk 2: Quality Control at Scale
**Risk:** Open marketplace could fill with low-quality or scammy skills.
**Mitigation:**
- Mandatory design system (everything looks premium)
- Curation layer (featured/verified badges)
- Review and rating system
- Quality gates before listing

### Risk 3: AI Cost Margins
**Risk:** Agent teams could have thin margins if AI costs spike.
**Mitigation:**
- Tiered agent teams (simpler = cheaper models)
- Usage-based pricing passes cost to heavy users
- Multi-provider strategy (arbitrage across OpenAI/Anthropic/open-source)
